LAN sharing

Type @, pick people, send. One step for everyone.

Select a lot, press Ctrl+Shift+Enter and mention colleagues or a whole team. Hopper finds everyone running it on the network by name.

  • Direct PC-to-PC over the office network: encrypted, parallel streams, resumes after a drop, every file checksum-verified.
  • Colleague offline? It waits in their server inbox and arrives when their Hopper starts.
  • Click a person to message them, send files, send a folder link or ask them for files. The office bot can DM them in Zoho Cliq.
Dexterity Share · redesigned in 2.6

Send a lot to a client with one link.

Too big for e-mail? Select the files, choose Create client link (Ctrl+Alt+L). The files stay on the office server; the client gets a link to a Dexterity download page.

  • A page in our own brand: the title, who sent it, your note and when the link closes, with one Download all button. A line from our office to the client shows every state, from downloading to done. Light or dark, on a desk or a phone.
  • Expires after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days. Optional password and download limit, and ready-made e-mail text that states the expiry date.
  • My client links (Share ▾ or Ctrl+Alt+K) shows how many times each link was downloaded; extend or revoke any time. Cliq tells you on the first download and before a link closes.
Dexterity Share · in a browser

No Hopper on that PC? Make the link in a browser.

On a site laptop or at home, open the Dexterity Share staff page, drop a finished .zip, .rar or .7z, choose the days, a password and a note, and get the link and the e-mail text.

  • Sign in once per browser from Hopper: My client links → Use in a browser → Make a sign-in link. Open it, bookmark the page; no password to remember.
  • Uploads go in pieces, with Pause, Resume and Cancel, and carry on after a dropped connection. Up to 5 GB by default.
  • Your links lists your links, from Hopper too, with downloads and expiry: Copy, Renew or Switch off. Switch a lost browser off from Hopper.

Copy, move, paste · new in 2.3

See every copy through, even on a network drive.

Copy, move, delete, zip and extract open a small progress window: percentage, items done, the current file, speed and time left. A name conflict is asked right there.

  • Pause, Cancel, or Hide to let it carry on in the background. “Do this for all conflicts” in one tick.

Office setup

A few minutes for the whole office.

  1. Install Hopper on each PC. Per-user, no admin rights, updates itself.
  2. Any PC, until the office is set up, in Settings → Office: create the office key and save the office config to the server. Choosing the always-on PC (Use this PC) locks the page to it, plus the standing admins; Revoke setup opens it again.
  3. Everyone else joins: from the shared config, an invite the admin sends over the network, or a pasted key. Then allow Hopper through the firewall and add your name.
  4. Dexterity Share, once, on a PC that stays on: three steps inside Hopper. No server software to install and no ports to open.

Questions

Good to know

Who can use Hopper?

Hopper is internal software of Dexterity Design Services Private Limited, for Dexterity staff and authorised contractors on office PCs.

Do files leave the office network?

Not when you share with colleagues: files go directly between office PCs over TLS with the office key, or through the office server inbox. A client link is the one case you choose: the files stay on the office server and are served from it only to people who have the link, until it expires. Only notification text goes to Zoho Cliq, and only when the bot is switched on.

Can I send files to a client?

Yes. Select the files, choose Create client link, pick how many days it lasts and, if you like, a password and download limit. Hopper gives you the link and the e-mail text. My client links shows when the client downloads, and lets you extend or revoke the link. Away from Hopper, upload a .zip, .rar or .7z on the staff page in your browser and get the link there.

How does the browser page know it is me?

Hopper makes a personal sign-in link (My client links → Use in a browser). Open it once and bookmark the page; that browser then remembers you. The server keeps only a fingerprint of the sign-in, never the sign-in itself. You can switch any browser off in Hopper, and you only ever see your own links.

What if a colleague's PC is off?

The share is copied to their folder in the server inbox. When their Hopper starts, it moves the files into their Inbox and notifies them.

Does it replace Windows Explorer?

It doesn't need it. The context menu, Properties, Open with, the folder picker and the Recycle Bin view are Hopper's own, and folders always open inside Hopper. It can also be your default for folders (Settings → Advanced). Copy and paste through the Windows clipboard and drag out to other apps still work.

Why does Windows warn the first time I install it?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen asks you to confirm. Each release lists SHA-256 checksums to verify the download.
